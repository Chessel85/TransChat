TransChat

Text chat between two people who speak different languages. You read and write in your own language; messages are translated for you.

Messages go directly between your two browsers. Translation is done on your own computer by your browser. Nothing is saved after you close the page.

      Keyboard shortcuts
      • Enter: send message. Shift+Enter: new line.
      • Alt+1 to Alt+9: read the most recent messages again (Alt+1 is the latest).
      • Alt+Shift+1 to Alt+Shift+9: read the original, untranslated text of that message.
      • Alt+P: who is here and what language they speak.
      • Alt+L: move to the message list to review it. Escape or Alt+M: back to the message box.
